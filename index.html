FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

IS REGISTRATION FREE?

Yes! Registration, joining our Telegram community and participating in live online tutorial classes are completely free.

WHICH COURSES ARE COVERED?

Our initial tutorial courses include PHY 101, CHM 101, MTH 101, CHM 107 and PHY 107.

WHO CAN PARTICIPATE?

Our campaign is specially designed for students in Science, Engineering, Medical Laboratory Science, Medical College, Education and other science-related disciplines.

WHICH PLATFORM DO YOU USE FOR TUTORIALS?

We use Telegram for live online tutorial classes and general student support. We also have different WhatsApp groups organized around Friendship Circles to help students connect, interact and support one another.

ARE THE LIVE ONLINE CLASSES FREE?

Absolutely! You can connect to and participate in our live online tutorial classes on Telegram without paying.

WILL I HAVE ACCESS TO RECORDED CLASSES?

Recorded tutorial sessions are included in the paid First Class Kit. Students who obtain the kit can access recordings through the Excel In School Portal.

WHAT IS THE FIRST CLASS KIT?

The First Class Kit is a special learning package designed to help serious students prepare effectively for their examinations and work towards achieving A's and B's in their courses.

WHAT IS INCLUDED IN THE FIRST CLASS KIT?

It includes Past Questions and Answers, Manual Filling Guides for all practical courses and Recorded Sessions of All Tutorial Classes.

HOW DO I ACCESS THE FIRST CLASS KIT?

You can purchase and access your First Class Kit through the Excel In School Portal.

IS THE EXCEL IN SCHOOL PORTAL FREE?

Live tutorials and general student support are free. However, access to the Excel In School Portal and the First Class Kit requires payment.

WHAT OTHER BENEFITS DO MEMBERS ENJOY?

Members can receive scholarship alerts, giveaways, brand ambassador opportunities, paid referral opportunities, early morning prayers and other student development opportunities.

WHAT ARE FRIENDSHIP CIRCLES?

Friendship Circles are WhatsApp groups that help students connect, interact and build supportive relationships with other members of Excel In School.

WHO FOUNDED EXCEL IN SCHOOL?

Excel In School was founded by Pastor Wisdom Ifeanyi of BLW RSU Fellowship, also known as Christ Embassy.
